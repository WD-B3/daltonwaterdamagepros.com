Water Damage Restoration in Murray Hill and Thornton Avenue, Dalton, GA

Murray Hill and Thornton Avenue hold some of the oldest houses in Dalton, on a slope where the city has built stormwater controls.

Water damage restoration in Murray Hill and Thornton Avenue means careful work in older homes with mature landscaping, west of the downtown core. The City of Dalton says construction in the district began in the 1840s and that most development came between 1937 and 1941. The houses are brick and clapboard in Queen Anne, Tudor and Colonial Revival styles.

If water is spreading, stop the source if you can and call. Tell dispatch the street, the room and how long the water has been running. In a historic house, how the work is done matters as much as how fast it starts.

What the city has built to manage water here

Murray Hill sits downhill from West Hill Cemetery. The city says a detention basin near Greenwood Drive and Emery Street has already been built in the neighborhood, and a series of step pools is planned on the slope above Evans Whitener Drive. The aim is to slow water and increase storage.

The city also finished streambank stabilization on Valley Drive, visible from Walnut Avenue near Thornton Avenue, to reduce downstream flooding near West Hill Cemetery. These are public projects aimed at runoff. They do not tell us how any single house has fared.

Historic houses need a careful plan

Older houses often have original wood floors, trim and plaster or other finishes that cost a lot to replace. A good crew identifies what the walls and floors are made of before anything is cut or removed. That conversation should happen with you, on site, before demolition begins.

Parts of Dalton are under local historic design review, which can affect exterior repairs. Ask the city whether your address is covered before you plan any change to the outside of the house. Drying and interior cleanup are a separate matter, and they should not wait.

Where water goes in an older two-story house

A leak in an upstairs bathroom can travel through the floor, down inside a wall and into a ceiling below. The stain on the ceiling is often far from the source. Moisture meters and thermal imaging help find the path without opening every wall.

Once the path is known, the crew dries it with air movers, dehumidifiers and sometimes drying mats on wood floors. See the hardwood floor page and the ceiling water damage page for what that looks like.

Insurance and records for a historic home

Coverage depends on the policy and the cause of the loss, so read it and ask your insurer. How a policy treats original materials varies, so ask the insurer before work starts. We cannot decide that and we do not promise coverage.

Photos, moisture readings and an itemized scope help describe the loss. If a feature is original, say so in the photos and the notes. The insurer can then see what was affected.

What to do first, before help arrives

  • Stay out of any room where water touches outlets, cords or a breaker panel, and shut off power at the panel only if you can reach it dry.
  • Stop the source if you can, such as the main valve or the appliance supply.
  • Move valuables and small furniture out of the wet area, and do not use a household vacuum on standing water.
  • Take dated photos before anything is moved, then call the 24/7 line.

Our timing advice follows the EPA guidelines on mold and moisture, which say wet materials should be dried within 24 to 48 hours. Claim steps follow the consumer guidance from the Georgia Office of Insurance and Safety Fire Commissioner.

Questions from Murray Hill homeowners

Do you handle water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Murray Hill?

Yes. The line is staffed all hours, nights and weekends included. Shut off the source if it is safe, then call before anything is cut or moved.

Does your service area include all of Murray Hill and Thornton Avenue?

Dispatch can confirm any address by phone, since we cannot quote a firm district line. Homes west of downtown around Thornton Avenue are inside the Dalton service area.

Can you work in a house in a historic district?

Interior water removal and drying is a normal part of the work. Exterior repairs in a locally designated district may need city design review, so ask the city before you change the outside.

Will you cut into my plaster or woodwork?

Only after testing shows it is needed, and the crew should explain the reason and the extent before any cutting. Ask for it in writing as part of the scope.

Has Murray Hill had stormwater problems?

The city built a detention basin near Greenwood Drive and Emery Street and plans more work above it. The city describes these as flood reduction projects, not a record of any single house.

Does insurance cover water damage in an older home?

It depends on the cause and the policy terms. Read the policy and ask the insurer, and keep photos and moisture records.

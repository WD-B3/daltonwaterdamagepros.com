What to have ready when you call

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street or landmark.
  • Where the water came from, if you know, and whether it is still running.
  • How deep the water is and which rooms it has reached.
  • Whether anything electrical, such as the panel, an outlet or an appliance, is wet.
  • Your insurance company, if you plan to file a claim. Do not worry if you do not have the policy number handy.

Photos help once the crew arrives. If you can do it safely, take pictures of the source, the water line and the damaged items before anything is moved.

If water is still spreading

Close the main water valve, keep clear of wet outlets and lift valuables off the floor. The water extraction page has a fuller checklist for the first hour.

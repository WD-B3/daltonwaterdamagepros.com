Where the Dalton crew works

Dispatch runs from Dalton, GA, the seat of Whitfield County, and the crew drives out to the towns around it. Dalton ZIP codes 30719, 30720, 30721 and 30722 are covered in full. Five nearby towns have their own pages, and each page uses facts that apply to that town only.

The Dalton area sits in the Dalton metropolitan area of northwest Georgia, between the ridges of the Cumberland Plateau to the west and the Cohutta Mountains to the east. Roads such as Interstate 75, US 41, US 76 and Georgia 52 tie the towns together, and they decide how the crew reaches you.

Rocky Face and the ridge side

Rocky Face lies west of the city at the foot of Rocky Face Ridge. Runoff from the slope is the local concern, and the Rocky Face page explains it.

Tunnel Hill, Varnell and Cohutta to the north

Three towns sit north and northwest of Dalton. Tunnel Hill straddles the Whitfield and Catoosa county line, Varnell is a growing family town and Cohutta runs along Cleveland Highway near the Tennessee line.

Chatsworth across the county line

Chatsworth is the seat of Murray County and sits about 13 miles east of Dalton on US 76. Permit offices there differ from Whitfield County, which is covered on the Chatsworth page.

Other places we get calls from

Crandall, Fort Oglethorpe, Resaca and Ringgold are outside the five town pages but within reach. Call the line, give the address and dispatch confirms the route and the plan.

The ground under the service area

Elevations run from about 745 feet at Chatsworth to about 863 feet at Cohutta, and the land between rises and falls with the ridges. Water follows that shape. Runoff moves down slopes near Rocky Face, collects along the creeks that feed the Conasauga River and fills low yards in town.

Those patterns decide which pages matter most in each town. Flooding and crawl space problems come up often near the creeks, and pipe and appliance failures come up everywhere. The flood damage page and the crawl space page cover the first two.

That means more homes, more rentals and more small businesses around the courthouse square. A pipe that fails in a rental can wet the unit below, and a leak in a shop can wet stock.

A railroad town in a creek valley

Chatsworth started in 1905 as a depot on the Louisville and Nashville Railroad, was incorporated as a town in 1906 and became a city in 1923. It sits on the west side of the Holly Creek valley, and Holly Creek is a tributary of the Conasauga River. Fort Mountain and Cohutta Mountain rise to the east.

With about 57.5 inches of rain a year, the area is wetter than much of Georgia, and mountain runoff moves toward the valley. In 2026, flooding in neighboring north Georgia counties led the governor to declare a state of emergency for Fannin, Gilmer, Murray and Union counties. What to do first, before help arrives

  • Stay out of any room where water touches outlets, cords or a breaker panel, and shut off power at the panel only if you can reach it dry.
  • Stop the source if you can, such as the main valve or the appliance supply.
  • Move valuables and small furniture out of the wet area, and do not use a household vacuum on standing water.
  • Take dated photos before anything is moved, then call the 24/7 line.

Our timing advice follows the EPA guidelines on mold and moisture, which say wet materials should be dried within 24 to 48 hours. Claim steps follow the consumer guidance from the Georgia Office of Insurance and Safety Fire Commissioner.

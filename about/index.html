Who answers the phone

Dalton Water Damage Pros is a 24/7 dispatch service for water damage in Dalton, Georgia and the towns around it. When you call, dispatch asks about the source, the depth and what is wet, then sends licensed and insured restoration professionals to the job. The company does not name individual technicians or show credentials it cannot back up.

That is a deliberate choice. A water loss is stressful, and the useful thing a website can do is tell you what to do next and what to expect. This site tries to do that with plain language and sources you can check.

What the service covers

The service covers the whole range of water damage in a home or a building, from a burst pipe at midnight to a slow crawl space problem found at a home inspection. There are twenty service pages, grouped on the services index, and a secondary page on mold remediation for losses that went untreated.

The industry references are the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage and the S520 standard for mold, and you can ask any company whether it follows them. You can ask any company, including this one, whether its crews work to those standards and what records they keep.

How we write the pages

Every page here is written for a reader in the middle of a loss. We use primary sources where they exist, such as the EPA for mold and moisture, the Georgia Office of Insurance and Safety Fire Commissioner for claims, FEMA for flood maps and the National Weather Service for rainfall and flood stages. Where a fact could not be confirmed from a primary source, we left it out.

Local detail matters too. A house near the Crown Mill Historic District dries differently from a new build in Varnell, and a home in the Chatsworth area faces different weather than one on the Dalton valley floor. Each town page, listed on the service area page, is written from facts that apply to that town only.

What you will not find here

  • Invented reviews, star ratings or case studies.
  • License numbers, certification counts or awards that we cannot show.
  • Promises about how fast a crew will arrive.
  • Claims that an insurer will approve or pay a claim.
  • Web forms. This is a call-only service, so you speak to a person.

If a page here ever claims something that does not match what happens on your call, trust the call and tell dispatch.

How a call goes

  1. Dispatch asks where the water came from and whether anything electrical is wet.
  2. You get safety steps to follow while the crew is on the way, such as closing the main valve.
  3. The crew arrives, confirms the scope and explains price before work starts.
  4. Extraction, drying and documentation follow, with moisture readings logged each day.
  5. You receive photos and readings that support repairs and any insurance claim.

Standards we hold the content to

  • A claim about a law, a code or a number needs a primary source or it is removed.
  • Costs are described by the factors that set them, never by a made-up price.
  • Mold pages leave health questions to a physician and never promise that growth will not return.
  • Insurance is explained in general terms only, with a reminder to read the policy and ask the insurer.
  • Emergency pages say what to do in the first hour, before they say anything about us.

The water extraction and burst pipe pages are good examples of the format. They begin with safety steps, move to how the work is done and end with insurance notes and questions.

Questions about the service

Is Dalton Water Damage Pros a restoration company or a dispatch service?

It is a 24/7 dispatch service. Calls are answered by dispatch and licensed and insured restoration professionals are sent to the job.

Do you list technician names or certifications?

No. The pages do not name individuals or claim certifications that cannot be shown. Ask any company that comes to your home what training its crew has and what records it keeps.

Can I book online?

No. There are no web forms. Calling puts you in touch with a person who can ask about the water and plan the equipment.

Email the address on the contact page and the page will be reviewed.
